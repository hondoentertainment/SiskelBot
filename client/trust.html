Security & trust

How Siskel Bot handles credentials, tenancy, payments, and operations. Hosted and self-hosted deployments vary by your configuration.

Secrets & backend access

  • API keys and OAuth tokens belong in environment variables or your vault — not in shipped client bundles for production builds.
  • Chat traffic is proxied to the LLM backend you configure (e.g. Ollama, vLLM, OpenAI). Prefer private networks between Siskel Bot and your inference tier when possible.

Multi-tenant data

  • Workspaces, conversations, knowledge, and usage are partitioned by workspace and user where the product tier supports it.
  • Enable per-workspace rate limits and plan quotas (QUOTA_ENABLED) when monetizing — usage is recorded for billing dashboards when chat completes successfully.

Billing

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Webhooks sync subscription state into workspace plans and tenant quotas so limits match what customers pay for.
  • See Pricing for plan limits; successful checkout returns via /billing/success.

Observability

  • Structured logs honor request IDs; Prometheus-style metrics may be exposed when configured (see RUNBOOK).
  • Review log redaction policies in your deployment checklist before passing production traffic.
