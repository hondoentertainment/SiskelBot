Privacy overview

How Siskel Bot treats end-user and customer data. Replace bracketed fields for your legal entity and jurisdiction before publishing as policy.

What we process

  • Chat & agent traffic — prompts, tool calls, and completions pass through the proxy to your configured LLM backend.
  • Product data — conversations, knowledge documents, workspace metadata, usage metrics, and webhook payloads you configure.
  • Accounts — OAuth profile identifiers and emails when social login is enabled; API key identifiers when key auth is used.
  • Billing — minimal subscription metadata via Stripe (customer id, status) stored to enforce plan tiers.

Your responsibilities (self-hosted)

  • Determine lawful basis and retention for data you store under STORAGE_PATH or Postgres.
  • Protect inference endpoints and API keys; rotate keys on a schedule.
  • Document subprocessors you enable (see Subprocessors as a starting checklist).

Hosted deployments

  • Publish a definitive privacy policy URL once you have vendor-specific terms.
  • Offer a DPA when selling to businesses — see DPA template.
This page is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Have counsel review before customer-facing use.
