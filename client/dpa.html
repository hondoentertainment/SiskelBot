Data Processing Agreement (template)

Short-form DPA outline for B2B buyers. Replace [CUSTOMER], [VENDOR], and [EFFECTIVE DATE], then have legal execute.

1. Definitions

  1. Controller / Processor — Customer is the controller of personal data submitted to the service; Vendor processes such data on documented instructions.
  2. Services — Hosted Siskel Bot deployment(s) identified in the order form or subscription.

2. Purpose & duration

Processing is limited to providing the AI gateway, storage, and collaboration features for the subscription term.

3. Subprocessors

Vendor maintains a current list (see Subprocessors) and will notify material changes where contractually required.

4. Security

Vendor implements commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures commensurate with risk, including access controls, encryption in transit where standard for web services, and configurable secret handling.

5. Assistance & deletion

Vendor assists with data subject requests to the extent technically feasible; Customer may export or delete workspace data through product features or deletion requests coordinated with Vendor support.

This template is informational. SOC 2 and EU SCC packages are roadmap items — negotiate specifics in enterprise orders.
